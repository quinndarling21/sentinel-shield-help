Scam alert: fake ParcelPoint $1.99 redelivery fee

Published Oct 5, 2026 · Sentinel Shield Research

A text says your package is on hold and asks for a $1.99 redelivery fee. The link goes to a fake ParcelPoint page that takes your card.

What the message looks like

The text claims to be from ParcelPoint. It says your package is on hold, or that a delivery attempt failed. It asks you to pay $1.99 to release it, sometimes within 24 hours. It may say "Reply STOP to opt out." The same scam also shows up on TikTok and X. The link opens a page that looks like ParcelPoint, with a fake tracking number, a countdown clock, and a Pay now button for $1.99.

How to spot it

  • Real delivery companies do not ask for a small redelivery fee by text from a random number.
  • The fee is tiny on purpose so you pay without thinking.
  • The link is not the real ParcelPoint website.
  • It rushes you with a 24-hour countdown.

If you already paid

  • Call your bank or card company now and tell them the payment was a scam.
  • Ask for a new card number.
  • Change any password you typed on the page.
  • Report the text: forward it to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.

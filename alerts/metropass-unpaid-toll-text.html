Scam alert: fake MetroPass unpaid toll text

Published Sep 23, 2026 · Sentinel Shield Research

A text says you owe a small toll and threatens a late fee. The link goes to a fake payment page that steals card details.

What the message looks like

The text claims to be from MetroPass Tolls. It says you have an unpaid toll of a few dollars and must pay today to avoid a $50 late fee. It links to a page that looks like a toll payment site.

How to spot it

  • Toll agencies send bills by mail or in their app, not by text from a random number.
  • The amount is small on purpose so you pay without thinking.
  • The link is not the official MetroPass website.
  • It rushes you with a deadline and a big late fee.

If you already paid

  • Call your bank or card company now and tell them the payment was a scam.
  • Ask for a new card number.
  • Change any password you typed on the page.
  • Report the text: forward it to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.

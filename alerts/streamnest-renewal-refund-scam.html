Scam alert: fake StreamNest renewal refund

Published Sep 30, 2026 · Sentinel Shield Research

A message says your StreamNest plan renewed for $89.99 and offers a refund. The refund page asks for your card.

What the message looks like

The message says your annual StreamNest plan just renewed and that you can cancel and get a refund. It sends you to a refund page or asks you to call a number.

How to spot it

  • You never signed up for an annual plan.
  • Real refunds go back to your card on their own. You never need to type your card in to get one.
  • The refund link is not the official StreamNest website.
  • The caller may ask to connect to your computer. Never allow it.

If you already paid

  • Call your bank or card company and report the charge.
  • If you let someone connect to your computer, run a full Sentinel Shield scan and change your passwords.
  • Report the message, then delete it.
🛡️
Sentinel Shield now blocks this link. If you or anyone on your plan opens the refund page, you will see a warning page instead.
